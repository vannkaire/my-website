Summary About Basketball

Basketball is a thrilling team sport that combines skill, strategy, and athleticism. Played on a rectangular court, the objective is to score points by shooting a ball through the opposing team's hoop, which is set 10 feet off the ground. The game is typically played by two teams of five players each, and it has gained immense popularity worldwide.

History

Invented in 1891 by Dr. James Naismith, basketball was initially designed as an indoor activity to keep students active during winter. The first official game was played with a soccer ball and two peach baskets, and it has evolved significantly since then. Today, basketball features professional leagues such as the NBA, international competitions like the FIBA World Cup, and college tournaments like March Madness.
